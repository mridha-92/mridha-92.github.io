Japanese manufacturer Iris Ohyama, operator of the Iris Plaza shopping platform, has disclosed a data breach that potentially exposed the personal information of approximately 7.7 million customers.

The exposure resulted from unauthorized access identified within a third-party cloud service leveraged by the company.

The root cause of the incident is unauthorized access to an external third-party cloud service utilized by Iris Ohyama.
