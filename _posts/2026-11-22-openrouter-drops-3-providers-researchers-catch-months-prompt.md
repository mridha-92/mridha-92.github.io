### Executive Summary
* Industrial cybersecurity firm Dragos revealed that attackers breached three AI inference providers and stole customer data from inside their environments over a period of months.
* None of the three affected providers were aware of the unauthorized access until security researchers notified them of the compromise.
* AI request gateway OpenRouter, which routes user requests across a network of host providers, subsequently dropped all three compromised inference providers from its network.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
* Attackers intercepted and exfiltrated user AI prompts sent to the compromised hosting firms over several months, compromising request privacy.
* The stolen data included prompts specifically designated under "Zero Data Retention" settings—a feature designed to guarantee that prompt data is never stored on provider systems.
* The specific root cause and initial access vectors utilized by the threat actors to gain undetected access to the providers' internal infrastructure are not yet confirmed.

### Mitigation & IOCs
* Direct technical mitigations from the source are not yet confirmed, though OpenRouter mitigates further exposure by severing ties with the three impacted AI inference providers.
