### Executive Summary

Over a four-day span ending 11 October 2026, three Yandex data centres were knocked offline or damaged by physical Ukrainian drone strikes. The targeted facilities include sites in the Ryazan, Kaluga, and Vladimir regions of Russia.

The physical damage caused a major drop in outbound network traffic across Yandex infrastructure. Cloudflare Radar metrics show traffic originating from Yandex's primary network collapsing by roughly 94% on 11 October, disrupting multiple consumer and commercial services.

Yandex has issued statements confirming full or partial shutdowns across the affected facilities, stating that it is currently uncertain when the damaged equipment will return to operational status.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The operational disruptions stem directly from targeted kinetic strikes on critical Yandex data infrastructure across three distinct locations:
* **Sasovo, Ryazan region (7–8 October):** The facility sustained severe damage and was completely shut down. The site houses two of the three supercomputers Yandex utilizes to train its AI models.
* **Kaluga region (9 October):** A portion of the facility's infrastructure was taken out of service.
* **Vladimir region (11 October):** Drone impacts forced a complete shutdown of the site.

Network telemetries published by Cloudflare Radar for Yandex's Autonomous System (AS13238) demonstrate a severe operational impact. At 18:00 UTC on 11 October, HTTP request volume dropped to 7% of the period's maximum, compared to 82.6% during the identical timeframe the previous week. The outage affects multiple Yandex services, including navigation and traffic applications, streaming music and video, grocery delivery processing, and enterprise business ordering tools.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Yandex has informed clients that restoring damaged equipment remains unpredictable due to the severity of physical site damage. Organizations relying on Yandex infrastructure and enterprise business tools should transition to alternative service providers or backup platforms until operations stabilize.
