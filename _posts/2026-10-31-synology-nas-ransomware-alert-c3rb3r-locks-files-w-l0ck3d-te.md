A ransomware alert has been issued regarding C3RB3R ransomware actively targeting Synology Network-Attached Storage (NAS) devices. 

The ransomware compromises target devices to encrypt user files, appending the `.L0CK3D` file extension to affected data. Broader details regarding attack campaigns or threat actor identity are not yet confirmed.
