### Executive Summary

Threat actors are actively deploying passkey-themed social engineering campaigns to compromise user identities and execute broader attacks against cloud environments.

According to Microsoft Security, successful social engineering enables malicious actors to establish persistent access and infiltrate organizational assets, posing a significant risk to cloud identity infrastructure.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

* **Root Cause:** Adversaries utilize social engineering tactics themed around passkeys to trick users into granting initial access or compromising their identity credentials.
* **Impact & Persistence:** Once access is obtained, threat actors establish Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) persistence to maintain access. They abuse Microsoft Graph to conduct internal reconnaissance and systematically access sensitive data stored across SharePoint, OneDrive, and email applications.

### Mitigation & IOCs

To counter passkey-themed identity threats, organizations should deploy detection and mitigation strategies focused on monitoring suspicious MFA registration changes, auditing Microsoft Graph API activity for unauthorized reconnaissance, and securing cloud data repositories.
