### Executive Summary
An Android banking app-cloning campaign has been detected targeting users in Indonesia. The threat activity involves multiple strains of mobile malware targeting the Android ecosystem.

The campaign features the GoldFactory threat group delivering the Gigabud Trojan. Additionally, the Mantax Otax malware strain is observed spreading separately within the region.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause of the primary delivery mechanism stems from the GoldFactory threat group abusing the legitimate Android Work Profile feature. Threat actors exploit this feature to facilitate the delivery and execution of the Gigabud Trojan on compromised devices.

The impact of this campaign centers on mobile banking risk, where threat actors utilize app-cloning techniques to compromise Android devices and potentially harvest sensitive user credentials and financial data.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Specific technical mitigations were not detailed in the provided source report. Organizations managing mobile fleets should enforce strict Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) controls and restrict unverified profile creations on Android devices.
