### Executive Summary

A security practitioner report published on Reddit highlights response delay challenges regarding the takedown of malicious sites hosted on Vercel's cloud platform (`*.vercel.app`). The post details an active phishing campaign remaining live more than a week after initial reports were submitted to the hosting provider's abuse team.

The reported campaign actively targets consumers by cloning a legitimate brand to harvest financial data. The issue reflects community concerns over abuse reporting workflows and resolution timelines when attempting to take down malicious deployments hosted on serverless and cloud hosting providers.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

**Root Cause:** Threat actors are utilizing Vercel's deployment infrastructure (`*.vercel.app`) to host unauthorized brand clones. Slow abuse report processing and ticket stalls via standard reporting channels (`abuse@vercel.com`) have allowed the malicious infrastructure to remain operational.

**Technical Impact:** The hosted phishing site implements a fraudulent checkout workflow paired with a fake 3D Secure (3DS) One-Time Password (OTP) prompt. This setup directly facilitates the illicit harvesting of credit card numbers and multi-factor payment authentication codes from victimized users.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Entities seeking to mitigate active phishing deployments on the platform while awaiting abuse processing should consider parallel reporting paths, including:
* Reporting malicious URLs to third-party reputation services such as Google Safe Browsing and Netcraft.
* Escalating takedown requests through formal trademark and brand protection channels rather than standard phishing abuse forms.
* Directing official abuse inquiries and evidence packages to `abuse@vercel.com`.
