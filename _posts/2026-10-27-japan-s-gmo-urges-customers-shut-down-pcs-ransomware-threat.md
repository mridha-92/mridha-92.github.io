### Executive Summary
Japanese company GMO Group has issued an urgent warning advising customers to disconnect their systems from the internet and power off their computers. The alert was issued after an unauthorized threat actor breached the company's Onamae Desktop Cloud environment.

The threat actor has threatened to launch a ransomware attack today targeting the compromised infrastructure and its users. GMO Group's recommendation to shut down machines aims to mitigate potential damage from an impending ransomware deployment.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause of the incident involves an unauthorized breach of GMO Group's Onamae Desktop Cloud service. Specific details regarding the initial access vector, vulnerable components, or the identity of the threat actor have not been disclosed.

If executed, the threatened ransomware deployment could lead to widespread data encryption and disruption across systems connected to the Onamae Desktop Cloud infrastructure. Further technical impact details remain unconfirmed.

### Mitigation & IOCs
GMO Group advises all affected customers to implement the following immediate countermeasures:
* Disconnect systems from the internet.
* Shut down all impacted computers connected to the Onamae Desktop Cloud environment.
