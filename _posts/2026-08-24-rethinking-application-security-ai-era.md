### Executive Summary
SecurityWeek reports that artificial intelligence is significantly accelerating the threat landscape by reducing the time between public vulnerability disclosure and active exploitation. 

As a result of these shortened threat windows, enterprises are forced to re-evaluate traditional application security models and adopt strategies that go beyond standard vulnerability patching to reduce application risk.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
* **Root Cause**: The integration of AI technologies enables threat actors to rapidly analyze security advisories, vulnerability disclosures, and code changes to automate the creation of functional exploits.
* **Technical Impact**: Traditional patching schedules are rendered insufficient due to the compressed timeframe between disclosure and weaponization. Organizations relying solely on reactive patch management face an elevated risk of initial access and application compromise before defensive updates can be deployed.
