### Executive Summary
Microsoft Security published guidance addressing security considerations as artificial intelligence deployments shift into customer-owned edge environments.

Organizations operating in these environments require new verification methods to evaluate untrusted systems, software, and AI assets. Establishing verified trust is necessary before releasing sensitive enterprise data, credentials, and machine learning models into edge infrastructure.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The transition of AI workloads into customer-owned environments exposes sensitive operational assets to potential risks if the underlying infrastructure is unverified. If edge systems and software are not properly validated, releasing high-value assets can compromise enterprise credentials, data, and proprietary AI models.

The underlying challenge stems from the lack of established verification controls tailored for edge AI components, necessitating strict validation of software and hardware trust prior to asset deployment.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Based on the provided guidance, organizations should apply the following mitigations:
* Develop and enforce verification mechanisms to validate systems, software, and AI assets before deployment.
* Restrict the release of sensitive data, credentials, and AI models until the underlying environment passes trust verification.
