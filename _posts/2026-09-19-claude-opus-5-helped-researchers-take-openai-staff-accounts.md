### Executive Summary

Three security researchers at the firm Hacktron utilized Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 AI model to identify and chain two separate security vulnerabilities targeting OpenAI's infrastructure. The activity was conducted as part of benign security research.

The vulnerability chain enabled the researchers to compromise the ChatGPT and Codex accounts of multiple OpenAI staff members. This unauthorized account access ultimately allowed the research team to reach an internal OpenAI code repository.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The attack vector originated with an initial bug located in the software powering OpenAI's public help forum. The researchers then leveraged Claude Opus 5 to link this initial vector to a secondary flaw residing within OpenAI's proprietary login system.

The technical impact of chaining these vulnerabilities includes full account takeover of staff-level ChatGPT and Codex accounts. The resulting privilege escalation granted access to internal corporate assets, specifically OpenAI's internal code repositories.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Specific vendor patch details and mitigations were not provided in the source material. Security teams are advised to audit external-facing forum software and enforce robust validation mechanisms within single sign-on and authentication flows to prevent vulnerability chaining.
