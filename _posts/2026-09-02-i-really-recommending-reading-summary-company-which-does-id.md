### Executive Summary
A third-party company responsible for identity verification during in-person interactions—such as hotel check-ins, car rentals, and age-verification for alcohol or marijuana purchases—has exposed a massive repository of personal data. The breach affects over 153,000,000 driver's licenses belonging to individuals across the United States.

The compromised dataset includes records associated with high-profile individuals, including Pete Hegseth. The exposed data is reportedly being offered for sale online for $100.
