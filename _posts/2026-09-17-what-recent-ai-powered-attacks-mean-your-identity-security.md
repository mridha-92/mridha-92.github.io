### Executive Summary

Advancements in artificial intelligence are enabling threat actors to accelerate and scale credential theft operations. This capability provides attackers with expanded opportunities to compromise and abuse legitimate user identities across enterprise environments.

According to cybersecurity vendor Specops, traditional identity security controls that rely strictly on successful authentication are no longer sufficient to defend against modern AI-driven attacks.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The root cause of this threat landscape shift is the leverage of AI technology to automate credential harvesting, making identity theft significantly faster and broader in scope.

The technical impact involves an increased rate of valid identity abuse. By utilizing stolen credentials, malicious actors can seamlessly navigate networks under the guise of legitimate accounts, rendering initial authentication mechanisms ineffective as a sole line of defense.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Organizations are advised to enhance identity security frameworks by looking beyond simple credential validation. Mitigation strategies should enforce continuous verification mechanisms that assess the trustworthiness of both the user identity and the device making the access request.
