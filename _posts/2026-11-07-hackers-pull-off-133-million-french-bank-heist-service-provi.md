### Executive Summary
French cooperative bank Crédit Mutuel Arkéa was targeted in a cyberattack routed through a service provider, facilitating unauthorized fraudulent transactions valued at up to $133 million.

In response to the incident, the institution implemented immediate containment protocols, including the suspension of specific systems to prevent additional unauthorized activity.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The breach leveraged a third-party service provider integration, granting attackers sufficient access to execute substantial fraudulent transactions across the bank's infrastructure. 

The technical root cause and specific vector used to compromise the third party are not yet confirmed. The primary operational impact includes the forced shutdown and suspension of affected banking systems while incident response efforts take place.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Reported mitigation measures taken by the target institution include suspending impacted systems to isolate the compromised third-party access point. Organizations are advised to enforce strict monitoring of third-party integration channels and temporarily isolate external service links exhibiting anomalous transactional activity.
