The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) has issued a warning regarding an AI-powered penetration testing framework called ARTEX, which malicious actors are actively using to automate cyberattacks against organizations globally.

The tool has been associated with recent high-impact security incidents, notably a ConnectWise supply-chain breach that led to the compromise of downstream Microsoft 365 customer environments.
