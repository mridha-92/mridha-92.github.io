### Executive Summary

A Kaspersky expert has discovered new Android malware targeting automotive infotainment systems. The malicious code is distributed through legitimate software designed for DoFun automotive head units.

The primary functions of this newly identified malware strain are serving unauthorized advertisements and enrolling compromised devices into a proxy botnet.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The root cause of infection involves the delivery of the malware via legitimate software packages associated with DoFun head units. The exact mechanism of initial software compromise remains unconfirmed in the initial report.

Upon successful installation on an Android-based DoFun head unit, the malware impacts device performance and network integrity. It forces the system to display advertisements and leverages the host connection to route external traffic, effectively transforming the infotainment unit into a node within a proxy botnet.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Organizations and users should audit software deployment pipelines for DoFun head units, ensure applications are sourced and verified through secure channels, and monitor network traffic originating from automotive infotainment systems for unauthorized proxy behavior.
