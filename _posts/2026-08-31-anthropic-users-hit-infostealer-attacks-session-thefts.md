### Executive Summary

An unidentified threat actor targeted users of Anthropic's Claude platform using multiple infostealer malware variants. The campaign resulted in session thefts and unauthorized account access.

The total number of affected users and compromised accounts remains unknown at this time. No specific threat actor group or infostealer strain has been officially attributed.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The root cause of the unauthorized account access stems from the deployment of infostealers that collected active session information from infected endpoints. 

By capturing valid session tokens, the threat actor was able to hijack user sessions and bypass standard authentication controls to access Claude accounts directly. Further technical details regarding specific infostealer malware families or distribution vectors are not yet confirmed.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Specific mitigation measures were not provided in the source report. General recommendations for addressing session hijacking include terminating active sessions, clearing stored tokens, and re-authenticating impacted user accounts.
