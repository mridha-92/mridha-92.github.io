### Executive Summary
Meta has announced new account security features for WhatsApp, introducing support for attaching multiple passkeys to a single account. This capability enables users with both iOS and Android devices to access their accounts using a phishing-resistant sign-in method.

According to Meta, more than 1 billion people currently use passkeys to log into WhatsApp. Passkey authentication was initially introduced on Android devices in October 2023, with this latest update expanding multi-device support across mobile platforms.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
Root Cause: Traditional authentication mechanisms remain vulnerable to social engineering and phishing attacks. To address these exposure vectors, Meta extended passkey functionality to support multiple registered passkeys per user account across operating systems.

Technical Impact: The expansion allows cross-platform users on iOS and Android to maintain strong, phishing-resistant authentication controls. By binding multiple passkeys to one account, users reduce reliance on weaker, interceptable sign-in mechanisms during account access across different devices.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Users on iOS and Android are advised to enable and configure passkeys within WhatsApp to enforce phishing-resistant authentication across all personal devices.
