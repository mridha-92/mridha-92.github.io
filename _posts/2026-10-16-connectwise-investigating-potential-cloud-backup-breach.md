### Executive Summary

ConnectWise is currently investigating reports of potential unauthorized access events targeting its Cloud Backup platform, formerly known as SkyKick. Official confirmation of a successful intrusion has not yet been issued by ConnectWise.

However, third-party threat intelligence sources report that a threat actor successfully compromised the backup platform and subsequently pivoted into the live Microsoft Office 365 (O365) environments of managed service provider (MSP) customers.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The root cause of the incident remains under investigation by ConnectWise, and official details regarding the specific attack vector are not yet confirmed. 

The potential technical impact is substantial because ConnectWise Cloud Backup is designed to perform full backups of O365 infrastructure, including Entra ID. If third-party reports are accurate, unauthorized access to the backup platform could allow adversaries to compromise sensitive backup files and pivot directly into live client tenant environments.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Specific vendor-provided mitigations have not yet been released. Managed service providers utilizing ConnectWise Cloud Backup are advised to monitor their live O365 and Entra ID environments for anomalous activity and continuously review updates on the official ConnectWise status page.
