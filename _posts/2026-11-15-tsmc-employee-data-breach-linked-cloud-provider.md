### Executive Summary

Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) has confirmed a data breach involving personal employee information leaked online. The incident came to light after a threat actor offered 6.9GB of data, allegedly exfiltrated from the company, for auction on an online platform. 

The compromise has been tied to an external cloud service provider used by the company. Cyberpent threat intelligence analysts are continuing to monitor the situation as further details regarding the full extent of the compromised data emerge.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The breach resulted in the unauthorized exposure of 6.9GB of employee personal data. Based on the initial confirmation, the incident stems from an issue associated with a third-party cloud provider rather than an direct compromise of TSMC's internal network infrastructure.

Detailed technical mechanisms regarding how the threat actor obtained access to the cloud environment, as well as the exact identity of the affected cloud vendor, have not yet been confirmed in the available source details.
