### Executive Summary
Cloud security company Wiz reported that threat actors actively exploited two chained vulnerabilities in self-hosted JFrog Artifactory servers between August 15 and September 8.

The attack campaign targeted software build pipeline repositories, allowing attackers to take full administrator control of vulnerable systems and plant persistent backdoors.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause stems from two security flaws within JFrog Artifactory that attackers chained together. The vulnerabilities specifically affect self-hosted installations that failed to apply vendor security patches.

Successful exploitation grants adversaries administrative privileges over the repository server. This compromises the build pipeline infrastructure and enables threat actors to deploy backdoors for persistent access.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Administrators of self-hosted JFrog Artifactory instances should ensure their servers are updated to the latest software release. JFrog had released patches resolving both flaws prior to the observed active exploitation window, so updated instances are protected.
