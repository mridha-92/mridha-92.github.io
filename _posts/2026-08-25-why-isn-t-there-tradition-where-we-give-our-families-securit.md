### Executive Summary

A community proposal published on Reddit CyberSec advocates for the implementation of structured, annual security checkups for family members ("Patchgiving") to mitigate preventable consumer-focused cyber breaches. The initiative addresses widespread end-user security gaps that frequently lead to compromised personal accounts, such as Google and WhatsApp.

The author emphasizes that non-technical users often lack awareness of basic defensive practices, leading to reactive requests for incident assistance only after compromise has occurred. The proposal suggests utilizing structured checklists to systematically remediate personal security postures.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

**Root Cause:**
The underlying cause of compromised user accounts is poor personal security hygiene and a lack of baseline technical awareness among non-technical users, leading to reliance on insecure fallbacks rather than proactive security controls.

**Technical Impact:**
Common vulnerabilities observed in personal environment audits include:
* Storage of cleartext credentials in unencrypted applications (e.g., Apple Notes).
* Credential reuse across multiple services combined with weak password creation.
* Absence of Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and failure to adopt modern authentication standards such as passkeys.
* Proliferation of unverified or obscure mobile applications accumulating unnecessary device permissions.
* Failure to apply routine system and application software updates.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Recommended mitigations derived from the source material include:
* Conduct periodic, structured security audits of personal and family devices.
* Migrate stored credentials from plain-text notes to dedicated password managers or passkeys.
* Enforce Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on critical accounts, specifically communication and primary identity providers (e.g., WhatsApp, Google).
* Review and remove obscure or unused mobile applications from end-user devices.
* Verify that automatic system and application security updates are enabled.
