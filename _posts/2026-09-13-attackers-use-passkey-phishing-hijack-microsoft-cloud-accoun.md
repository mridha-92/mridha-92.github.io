### Executive Summary
Microsoft has disclosed details regarding two cyber campaigns targeting third-party email delivery infrastructure and cloud environments. The initial campaign involved threat actors sending over one million financial fraud scam emails between August 3 and August 5, 2026, by spoofing chief executive officers.

A second parallel campaign used passkey-themed social engineering lures to compromise targets, hijack Microsoft cloud accounts, and exfiltrate data.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The first campaign leveraged third-party email delivery infrastructure to bypass delivery controls and distribute high-volume financial fraud messages masquerading as executive leadership. 

The second campaign targeted authentication mechanisms through passkey-themed social engineering. By deceiving users into granting access, threat actors gained unauthorized control over Microsoft cloud tenants and subsequently exfiltrated sensitive organizational data.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Organizations should review email authentication policies, audit third-party email delivery services, and educate users on social engineering lures related to passkeys and executive impersonation.
