### Executive Summary
E-commerce platform Cleverbridge has issued a warning regarding a data breach following an incident in which unauthorized hackers accessed its internal systems.

During the compromise, threat actors exfiltrated sensitive customer financial information, specifically full credit card numbers and Card Verification Value (CVV) security codes.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The compromise of both credit card numbers and CVV security codes significantly elevates the risk of fraudulent online transactions and financial misuse for affected users. 

Specific technical details concerning the threat actor's initial access vector, lateral movement, or the precise vulnerability exploited to breach Cleverbridge systems are not yet confirmed.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Impacted customers should closely monitor their financial and bank statements for unauthorized charges, report suspicious activity immediately to their card issuers, and consider requesting replacement payment cards.
