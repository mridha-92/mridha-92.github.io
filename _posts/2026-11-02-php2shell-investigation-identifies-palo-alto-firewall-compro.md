### Executive Summary
Follow-up reporting on the PHP2Shell exploit-chain investigation has identified Palo Alto Networks firewalls as active targets. This development provides specific product context to findings previously discussed by Dream Research regarding the exploit chain.

Palo Alto Networks has acknowledged limited exploitation affecting its customers. Research assessments suspect potential China-linked attribution; however, this assessment remains unverified and is not an independently established fact.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The threat actors utilize a multi-stage attack vector that combines PHP vulnerabilities with a Linux kernel privilege-escalation flaw. 

By chaining these vulnerabilities, attackers can elevate privileges and achieve full root access on compromised firewalls. Firewalls with exposed management interfaces represent the primary surface targeted in these exploitation efforts.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Administrators responsible for exposed firewall management interfaces should review official Palo Alto Networks advisories immediately and perform targeted investigations to check for potential compromise.
