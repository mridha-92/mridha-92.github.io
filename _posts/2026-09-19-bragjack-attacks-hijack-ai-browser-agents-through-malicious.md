### Executive Summary
A proof-of-concept attack named "BragJack" demonstrates how a single malicious browser extension can hijack AI assistants across multiple web browsers. Developed by researcher Gal Weizman of Forever Security, the attack technique leverages a vector termed "Prompt Forcing" to compromise AI integrations.

The vulnerability affects AI agents within Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera Neon, Perplexity Comet, and Claude in Chrome. The research yielded two CVE assignments and earned over $20,000 in bug bounties.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause of the BragJack attack relies on Prompt Forcing, a method where a malicious browser extension manipulates the interaction flow between the user, the browser environment, and the underlying AI assistant.

By leveraging extension privileges, the attack hijacks AI browser agents to execute unauthorized operations. Affected platforms include AI tools and extensions operating within Chrome, Edge, Opera Neon, Perplexity Comet, and Claude in Chrome.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Specific vendor mitigations are not detailed in the source material. Organizations and users are advised to restrict unverified browser extensions and monitor vendor updates for patches addressing Prompt Forcing in AI browser integrations.
