### Executive Summary

Cloudflare's Q3 2026 threat metrics reveal that threat actors are executing approximately 8,226 network-layer (L3) distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks every hour. This activity translates to roughly 197,400 attacks per day, representing a frequency of one attack occurring every 0.44 seconds.

During the third quarter of 2026, Cloudflare autonomously mitigated a total of 18.2 million network-layer DDoS attacks—a 38% increase—alongside 14.4 trillion HTTP DDoS requests aimed at web infrastructure.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The reported telemetry demonstrates sustained, high-frequency DDoS activity targeting both network infrastructure (L3) and application-layer protocols (HTTP). The sheer volume of 18.2 million network-layer attacks and 14.4 trillion HTTP requests highlights an ongoing reliance by threat actors on automated attack infrastructure.

Specific root causes, underlying vulnerabilities, botnet profiles, or targeted industry sectors were not specified in the provided reporting. Autonomous mitigation systems absorbed the malicious volume at the network edge.
