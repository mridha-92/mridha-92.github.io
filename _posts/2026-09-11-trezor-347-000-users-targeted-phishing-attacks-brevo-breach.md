### Executive Summary
Hardware cryptocurrency wallet vendor Trezor has disclosed a widespread phishing campaign that targeted 347,000 customer email addresses. Out of the targeted users, approximately 2,500 individuals clicked an embedded malicious link contained within the fraudulent messages. 

The phishing activity was initiated following a security breach at Brevo, a third-party email marketing platform utilized by Trezor. Cyberpent is monitoring the situation for further developments regarding potential wallet compromises or data exposure resulting from the incident.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause of the incident was a supply-chain compromise involving Brevo, a third-party email service provider. Unauthorized access to Brevo allowed threat actors to access customer email contact lists and distribute unauthorized phishing lures directly to Trezor users.

The primary technical impact involves deceptive social engineering via email, designed to redirect recipients to external malicious infrastructure. A total of 2,500 users interacted with the malicious links embedded in the communications, exposing them to potential credential theft or seed phrase compromise.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Trezor users are advised to exercise heightened caution with email communications claiming to originate from the company. Standard security best practices include:
* Verifying email senders and avoiding interaction with embedded links in unverified emails.
* Never entering hardware wallet recovery seeds or credentials on external websites or digital forms.
* Communicating directly through official support channels to verify account alerts.
