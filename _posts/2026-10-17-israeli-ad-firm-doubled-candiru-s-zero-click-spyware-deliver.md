### Executive Summary
An investigation reveals that Israeli advertising company Discover-Tech served as a zero-click spyware delivery mechanism associated with the spyware vendor Candiru. 

Advertisements distributed through Discover-Tech's platform allowed threat actors to track target devices and perform targeted infections without requiring user interaction.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause stems from the abuse of Discover-Tech's advertising distribution channels. Threat actors leveraged these ad networks to conduct large-scale fingerprinting and tracking of user devices.

Once target devices were identified and fingerprinted through the ad infrastructure, attackers delivered zero-click spyware to compromise the selected targets.
