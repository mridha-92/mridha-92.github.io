### Executive Summary
Cryptocurrency hardware wallet provider Trezor reported that approximately 347,000 of its users received phishing emails following a security breach at Brevo, a third-party email marketing platform. 

Threat actors compromised Brevo's platform and leveraged that access to distribute fraudulent emails. The unauthorized campaign targeted customers across multiple cryptocurrency-related platforms, including Trezor, BitBox, and CoinTracking.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause of the incident is a compromise of the Brevo marketing platform by unauthorized threat actors. By gaining access to Brevo's infrastructure, the attackers were able to send email communications directly through trusted distribution channels.

The primary impact involved the widespread dissemination of phishing emails to users of Trezor, BitBox, and CoinTracking. Because the messages were dispatched via an authorized marketing platform, the campaign posed an increased risk of tricking recipients.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Impacted organizations and users should remain vigilant against suspicious email communications claiming to come from Trezor, BitBox, or CoinTracking. Users should avoid clicking embedded links or sharing sensitive information such as recovery seeds or passphrases.
