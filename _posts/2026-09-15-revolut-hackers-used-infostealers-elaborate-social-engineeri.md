### Executive Summary

Threat actors targeted financial service Revolut using a combination of infostealer malware logs and elaborate social engineering techniques. Initial access was established through compromised government email accounts belonging to the Italian Ministry of the Interior (`pec.interno.it`). 

According to threat intelligence findings from Hudson Rock, approximately 300 compromised `pec.interno.it` webmail logins were identified in cybercrime databases from previously infected systems. Analysts assess that the attackers did not directly infect the targets themselves, but instead purchased or acquired pre-existing infostealer logs to obscure their initial access vector.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

* **Root Cause:** Compromised credentials harvested from historic infostealer malware infections provided attackers with unauthorized entry to legitimate government webmail portals.
* **Tactics & Persistence:** After accessing targeted email accounts, threat actors modified account security controls by registering recovery emails under their own operation, establishing logging mechanisms, and silently monitoring target communications.
* **Impact:** Adversaries leveraged legitimate `pec.interno.it` infrastructure to conduct high-credibility social engineering communications with Revolut, using genuine government email addresses to bypass standard trust boundaries.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Organizations should immediately audit webmail tenant configurations for unauthorized recovery emails or suspicious forwarding rules. Additionally, enterprise security teams must enforce robust Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and proactively monitor threat intelligence platforms for compromised employee credentials circulating in infostealer logs.
