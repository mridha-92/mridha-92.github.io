### Executive Summary
ConnectWise has shut down customer access to its Cloud Backup service (formerly known as SkyKick Cloud Backup) following a supply-chain cyberattack.

The incident occurred after threat actors gained unauthorized access to application identities within the service, enabling them to breach customer Microsoft 365 tenants.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause involves compromised application identities utilized by the ConnectWise Cloud Backup service. Attackers accessed these identities to bypass security boundaries and compromise downstream Microsoft 365 environments.

The technical impact includes unauthorized access to customer Microsoft 365 tenants and the operational disruption of the Cloud Backup service, which ConnectWise took offline to contain the threat.

### Mitigation & IOCs
As a primary mitigation measure, ConnectWise disabled customer access to the impacted Cloud Backup platform to prevent further unauthorized access to Microsoft 365 tenants.
