### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers have disclosed details regarding a large-scale malicious campaign targeting exposed enterprise network edge devices, specifically Fortinet FortiGate and Palo Alto Networks GlobalProtect firewalls.

According to reported details, the campaign has resulted in the exposure of over 70,000 credentials. The threat actors have successfully breached network perimeters to gain unauthorized access to numerous organizations worldwide, followed by attempts to further compromise victim networks.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause involves the targeting of internet-exposed edge devices and virtual private network (VPN) firewalls, which serve as critical entry points for enterprise environments.

Investigation artifacts indicate that operators leveraged compromised perimeter devices to systematically harvest credentials and gain footholds inside affected networks. Once initial access was established, the attackers attempted further exploitation against impacted organizations globally.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Specific remediation instructions were not provided in the source report. Organizations operating Fortinet FortiGate or Palo Alto Networks GlobalProtect devices should audit edge device access logs, restrict management interfaces from public exposure, and monitor for unauthorized access attempts.
