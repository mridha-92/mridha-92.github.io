### Executive Summary
U.S. government agencies have issued an advisory warning that threat actors are actively targeting industrial refrigeration and cold-chain monitoring systems worldwide. These malicious operations aim to cause widespread service disruptions, inventory destruction, and physical fire hazards.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause of these incidents involves adversaries targeting unpatched vulnerabilities or misconfigurations in internet-accessible industrial controllers. 

By taking control of these unmanaged or exposed interfaces, remote threat actors alter operational settings to force refrigeration units into continuous heating cycles. This unauthorized manipulation leads to severe inventory loss in cold-chain environments and creates physical safety risks, including fire hazards.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Organizations relying on industrial refrigeration systems should reduce risk by remediating software weaknesses in controllers and removing industrial control systems from direct internet visibility.
