### Executive Summary

JetBrains has reported a security incident from last month involving an unauthorized breach of its environment, specifically affecting JetBrains Cadence. Unidentified threat actors exploited a recently disclosed critical vulnerability in TeamCity to compromise the environment and extract AWS credentials.

In response to the compromise, JetBrains is urging all Cadence users to immediately revoke and rotate all credentials and secrets associated with their Cadence executions to prevent secondary unauthorized access.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The root cause of the incident stems from an unpatched TeamCity instance containing a recently disclosed critical vulnerability, which threat actors successfully leveraged to gain unauthorized access to JetBrains' internal environment.

The technical impact includes the breach of the JetBrains Cadence environment and the successful extraction of AWS credentials by the threat actors. Any secrets or credentials used during Cadence executions during or prior to the incident may be compromised.

### Mitigation & IOCs

JetBrains recommends the following response measures for affected organizations:

* Immediately revoke and rotate all AWS credentials, secrets, and access keys that may have been used within Cadence executions.
* Audit environment logs and access histories for any suspicious activity involving Cadence-related credentials.
* Verify that all internal and external TeamCity deployments are fully updated against recently disclosed vulnerabilities.
