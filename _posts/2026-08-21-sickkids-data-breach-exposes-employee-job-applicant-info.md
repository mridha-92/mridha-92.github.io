### Executive Summary

Toronto's Hospital for Sick Children (SickKids) has reported a cybersecurity incident that compromised the personal information of certain individuals associated with the organization. The exposed data belongs to some current and former employees, as well as job applicants.

The incident was isolated to non-clinical environments. SickKids confirmed that core clinical systems and patient medical records were not impacted or accessed during the event.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The breach originated from a security flaw present in a third-party software application used by the hospital. Exploitation of this third-party vulnerability allowed unauthorized access to internal administrative or HR-related information.

The technical impact remains limited to exposed personal data of personnel and applicants. Hospital operations and patient care systems maintained integrity throughout the incident.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Specific vendor mitigation instructions and patch details were not provided in the source report. Organizations are advised to maintain continuous visibility and patch management oversight across all third-party software solutions.
