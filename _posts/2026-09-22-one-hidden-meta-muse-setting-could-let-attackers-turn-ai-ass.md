### Executive Summary
Security researcher Patrick Wardle disclosed a proof-of-concept on September 21, 2026, demonstrating how malware already executing on a Mac can quietly compromise Meta's Muse assistant. 

The attack technique enables local malware to abuse the broad system permissions granted to the application by the user, effectively turning the AI assistant into a backdoor.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The exploit leverages a hidden setting within the Meta Muse application on macOS that can be modified by local malware without user interaction.

When a user taps the microphone and dictates a prompt, the modified setting redirects the dictated words to an attacker instead of Meta. This allows adversaries to covertly eavesdrop on voice prompts and exploit the application's trusted context on the host.

Vendor patches and specific mitigations are not yet confirmed in the provided source. Defense teams should employ robust endpoint detection and response (EDR) solutions to prevent initial malware execution and monitor unauthorized modifications to application settings on macOS devices.
