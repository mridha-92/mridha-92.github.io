### Executive Summary

Threat actor group FulcrumSec claims to have breached Manchester Airports Group (MAG) and exfiltrated 86 GB of sensitive data. Independent validation by BleepingComputer confirmed the authenticity of at least one traveller's record within the leaked data samples.

The exposed data contains detailed customer, booking, and travel information. This data scope extends beyond what MAG initially disclosed regarding the incident, indicating a potentially broader compromise of passenger data.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The breach resulted in the unauthorized exfiltration of 86 GB of data, impacting customer records, booking management details, and travel itineraries. The exposure of this information poses risks of targeted phishing, identity theft, and social engineering against affected travellers.

The root cause of the breach and the specific attack vectors used by FulcrumSec to gain unauthorized access to MAG's systems are currently not yet confirmed.
