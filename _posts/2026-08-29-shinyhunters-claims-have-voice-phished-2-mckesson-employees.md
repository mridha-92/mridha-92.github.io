### Executive Summary

Threat actor group ShinyHunters claims to have compromised healthcare company McKesson following a targeted voice-phishing (vishing) campaign against two employees. McKesson has confirmed the occurrence of a breach, but has not officially verified the full scope or severity of the incident.

ShinyHunters claims to have extracted approximately 284 million patient records during the intrusion. The compromised data allegedly includes predictive health data, personal identity details, and healthcare identifiers.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The initial root cause claimed by the threat actor is social engineering via voice-phishing targeting two McKesson employees. This method was allegedly leveraged to gain unauthorized access to internal systems containing sensitive patient databases.

The technical impact reported by ShinyHunters involves the unauthorized exfiltration of 284 million records. The exposure of predictive health data and healthcare identifiers poses significant regulatory and privacy risks, though the precise systems impacted remain unconfirmed by McKesson.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Organizations should defend against voice-phishing tactics by implementing robust identity verification controls for out-of-band requests, enforcing phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication (MFA) such as FIDO2 tokens, and conducting security awareness training focused on phone-based social engineering.
