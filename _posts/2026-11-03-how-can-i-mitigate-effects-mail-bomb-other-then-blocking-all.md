### Executive Summary
An organization is experiencing a sustained mail bomb attack targeting specific employees that has persisted for over three months. In response, the organization's security team implemented strict AntiSpam appliance controls to reduce incoming mail volume and maintain operational usability for the affected personnel.

Due to the prolonged duration of the attack, the security team is evaluating supplementary mitigation tactics. This includes exploring automated bulk unsubscribe utilities and on-premises software options to clear unwanted subscriptions without exposing enterprise data to third-party providers.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
*   **Root Cause**: Target enterprise email addresses are being subjected to a sustained high-volume mail bomb attack consisting primarily of graymail and automated newsletter sign-ups.
*   **Operational Impact**: Affected employees have been placed under a separate, restrictive anti-spam enforcement policy that blocks all graymail and newsletters. While this filters out the attack traffic, it completely prevents affected users from receiving legitimate newsletter communications.
*   **Persistence**: The malicious flood of incoming messages has continued for over three months, creating long-term administrative overhead and ongoing communication disruptions for impacted staff.

### Mitigation & IOCs
*   Deploy targeted policies on AntiSpam appliances to filter graymail and newsletter traffic for affected user accounts.
*   Evaluate automated bulk unsubscribe software, prioritizing self-hosted or on-premises solutions to prevent sending enterprise inbox data to external vendors.
