### Executive Summary

Enterprise Security Operations Centers (SOCs) have observed a rapid increase in security alerts generated by internal AI tools and agents over the past year. This emerging alert volume represents one of the fastest-growing categories within enterprise event streams.

The observed activity does not stem from external attacks against AI infrastructure. Instead, it reflects the operational footprint of routine organizational AI usage, including software developers running AI coding agents and non-technical staff authenticating consumer AI services using corporate credentials.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The root cause of this telemetry surge is the broad adoption of AI technologies across both technical and non-technical business units. As personnel integrate AI agents and third-party consumer tools into daily workflows, these systems interact with enterprise environments and trigger baseline security rules.

The technical impact primarily affects SOC monitoring efficiency. The volume of benign events generated by authorized or shadow AI usage creates noise within security information and event management systems, requiring analysts to differentiate standard operational AI activity from genuine threats.
