### Executive Summary
Fujitsu has launched a retainer-based incident response service in Japan utilizing a threat-analysis system powered by frontier AI. The service provides 24/7 coverage for forensic investigations, malware analysis, and suspicious-URL evaluation.

The operational model combines automated AI threat processing with mandatory review by human security specialists. Fujitsu experts verify all AI-generated findings and assume control of triage and containment when required, delivering threat-analysis reports in as little as one business day and forensic reports within two business days.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The technical workflow relies on a dedicated portal through which customers upload suspicious files and device telemetry. The frontier AI threat-analysis engine processes submitted data 24/7 to analyze malware, evaluate URLs, and perform preliminary forensic investigations.

The system is designed as an AI-assisted response model with human-in-the-loop validation rather than an fully autonomous replacement for incident response personnel. The AI accelerates initial data processing, while human security analysts serve as the validation layer to oversee triage, containment, and final report delivery.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Recommended measures based on the announcement include establishing retainer-based incident response protocols and utilizing secure, dedicated portals for uploading suspicious files, URLs, and device data to expert teams for rapid investigation and containment.
