### Executive Summary
A user reported a suspected Windows 11 host compromise following unauthorized promotional activity on their Instagram and Discord accounts. The impacted user observed unauthorized Instagram Story posts and unauthorized Discord messages sent to contacts, despite login activity logs showing no unfamiliar external devices.

The affected host displays symptoms of malware persistence and system tampering, including unresponsive local security software and abnormal startup entries. The user has isolated the device from the network and updated credentials for key accounts while seeking diagnostic steps.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
* **Technical Impact:** Unauthorized account access was used to distribute promotional material across Discord and Instagram. On the local host, Windows Defender is rendered unable to launch scans after UAC authorization, and PowerShell WMI/CIM execution returns errors, indicating potential security control suppression.
* **Observed System Artifacts:** Inspection of Windows Startup Apps revealed two suspicious entries: one labeled "0" and another displaying a GUID-like string. Local account auditing showed no unauthorized administrator accounts created, and Remote Desktop has since been disabled.
* **Root Cause:** Not yet confirmed. The symptoms align with potential infostealer activity or local session token abuse originating directly from the host.

### Mitigation & IOCs
* **Network Isolation:** Disconnect the affected system from local networks and the internet to stop potential data exfiltration or malware updates.
* **Credential & Session Reset:** Change passwords for associated accounts (such as Gmail, Instagram, and Discord) and terminate active sessions using a separate, uncompromised device.
* **System Remediation:** Refrain from running unverified remediation scripts. Perform targeted diagnostic logging or proceed with a clean Windows reinstallation to guarantee threat removal.
