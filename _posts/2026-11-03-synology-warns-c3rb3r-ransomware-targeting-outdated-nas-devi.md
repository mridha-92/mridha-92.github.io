### Executive Summary
Synology has issued a security warning regarding active threat activity involving C3RB3R ransomware. The campaign specifically targets outdated Network Attached Storage (NAS) devices.

Organizations operating Synology NAS hardware are advised that unpatched systems are currently at risk of compromise by operators deploying this ransomware strain.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The root cause identified in the advisory is the execution of outdated software on Synology NAS devices. Threat actors leverage these legacy system versions to gain initial access and deploy the C3RB3R ransomware.

Successful attacks result in the deployment of ransomware payloads across vulnerable NAS environments, leading to potential data unavailability, file encryption, and disruption of storage services.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Administrators should immediately update all Synology NAS devices to the latest supported operating system and firmware versions to remediate vulnerable software components. Ensure robust data backup policies and isolate non-updated storage appliances from exposed network segments.
