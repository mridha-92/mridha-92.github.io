### Executive Summary
A cyber threat operation referred to as "Spring Ring" is executing voice phishing (vishing) attacks targeting Microsoft Teams users. The campaign focuses on compromising users of the collaboration platform to establish initial access within targeted environments.

The primary objectives of the operation include gaining unauthorized remote access to user sessions, facilitating the spread of malware, and potentially escalating access to take over underlying infrastructure.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
**Root Cause:** The threat actors utilize vishing (voice phishing) tactics directed at Microsoft Teams users to bypass standard boundary defenses and compromise user accounts.

**Technical Impact:** Once a session is compromised, attackers gain remote access capabilities to the victim's account. This access enables the threat group to distribute malware laterally across the collaboration suite and work toward a broader takeover of organizational infrastructure.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Specific mitigations were not provided in the source report. Organizations utilizing collaboration platforms like Microsoft Teams should train personnel to identify and report suspicious voice phishing attempts.
