### Executive Summary
Cybersecurity researchers have reported an active cyber campaign in which unknown threat actors leveraged a compromised Microsoft 365 (M365) credential. The compromised account belongs to ConnectWise Cloud Backup (formerly SkyKick), a cloud-based backup and recovery service tailored for managed service providers (MSPs).

The threat actors utilized this single compromised credential to sign in and gain unauthorized access to 173 Microsoft 365 customer tenants. The identity and background of the threat actors remain unknown.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The primary root cause of the incident is the compromise of an M365 credential tied to ConnectWise Cloud Backup. Due to the trusted nature and integration of cloud backup platforms across customer M365 deployments, access to this credential enabled authentication into victim environments.

The impact spans 173 separate M365 customer tenants affected by unauthorized sign-ins. Specific post-exploitation activities, such as data tampering or exfiltration within the compromised M365 tenants, are not yet confirmed.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Affected organizations and MSPs relying on ConnectWise Cloud Backup should review authentication logs for third-party service connections, audit access permissions, and secure all credentials associated with cloud integrations.
