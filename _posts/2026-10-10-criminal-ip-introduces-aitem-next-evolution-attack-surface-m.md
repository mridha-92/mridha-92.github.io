### Executive Summary

Cybersecurity vendor Criminal IP has introduced AITEM, an AI-powered platform designed as the next iteration of Attack Surface Management (ASM). The announcement highlights a shift from basic asset discovery toward integrated threat analysis and remediation.

While traditional ASM tools provide visibility into exposed assets, Criminal IP asserts that discovery alone is insufficient for modern threat landscapes. AITEM addresses this gap by combining asset exposure identification with contextual investigation, risk prioritization, and incident response capabilities.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The underlying problem addressed by AITEM is the operational gap between asset discovery and actionable intelligence in traditional ASM implementations. Conventional discovery methods often generate large volumes of asset data without offering automated context or clear risk scoring, overburdening security teams.

AITEM leverages artificial intelligence to bridge this gap, integrating exposed asset telemetry directly with automated investigation workflows. By correlating exposure data with threat intelligence, the platform allows security analysts to prioritize high-risk assets and streamline response actions.

### Mitigation & IOCs

Organizations seeking to improve their attack surface management posture should consider adopting solutions that integrate exposure discovery with automated risk prioritization and threat response capabilities to reduce mean time to remediate (MTTR).
