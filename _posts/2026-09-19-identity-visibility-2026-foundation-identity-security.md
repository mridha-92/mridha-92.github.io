### Executive Summary

Identity visibility has emerged as a fundamental component of modern identity security strategies in 2026. Stolen and misused credentials remain among the most frequently reported initial access vectors highlighted in industry breach research, including Verizon's annual Data Breach Investigations Report.

As enterprise infrastructure shifts toward cloud and multicloud environments, managing and securing user identities grows increasingly complex. Establishing comprehensive identity visibility within Identity and Access Management (IAM) frameworks is vital for identifying blind spots and reducing credential-based exposure.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause

The primary driver behind identity-based compromises is the lack of centralized visibility across expanding cloud and multicloud environments. Decentralized identity ecosystems obscure active credentials, excess permissions, and unmonitored accounts, providing threat actors with opportunities for unauthorized access.

The technical impact of inadequate identity visibility includes undetected initial access and lateral movement via compromised credentials. Without unified visibility across IAM platforms, security teams cannot effectively detect credential abuse or enforce precise access controls.

### Mitigation & IOCs

To improve identity posture, organizations must implement solution capabilities that provide end-to-end identity visibility across all local, cloud, and multicloud environments. Security teams should regularly audit IAM configurations and continuously monitor credential usage to detect anomalies.
