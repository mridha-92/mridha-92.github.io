### Executive Summary
Google and Mozilla have issued software updates addressing more than 180 security vulnerabilities across their web browser platforms. 

The updates resolve 83 flaws in Google Chrome and 99 bugs in Mozilla Firefox, significantly reducing the attack surface for enterprise environments using these browsers.
