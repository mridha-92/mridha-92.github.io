### Executive Summary
American blockchain analysis firm Chainalysis has confirmed a security breach after an external threat actor gained unauthorized access to internal tools and exposed non-sensitive customer identity data.

According to the company, the incident was detected this month and involved an external actor utilizing offensive agentic technology. Chainalysis confirmed that the breach did not result in unauthorized access to investigation content, authentication credentials, or session tokens.

### Technical Impact & Root Cause
The breach stemmed from an intrusion into Chainalysis's internal tools by an external actor leveraging offensive agentic capabilities. 

The technical impact was restricted to the exposure of non-sensitive customer data. Critical investigative data and sensitive security tokens—including active session tokens and customer authentication credentials—were not accessed or compromised during the intrusion.

### Mitigation & IOCs
Chainalysis detected the external actor's activity and confirmed that investigative features and core security credentials remain secure. Specific remediation steps and technical mitigations were not disclosed in the provided report.
